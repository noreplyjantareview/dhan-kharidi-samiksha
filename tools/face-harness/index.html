Harness only — the phone has no buttons

Register is the enrolment at On Duty, Verify is the recognition at a later gate. Finish a registration, press Verify and read the cosines; then press Register with someone else in front of the camera and Verify against their face. That second pair is the number the threshold needs.

Calibration readout

Cosine of this frame's shape print against each stored sample. The engine accepts only when the second best of five and the centroid of five both reach the threshold.
