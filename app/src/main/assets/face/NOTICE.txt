Role: the only model the face check runs. It supplies 478 face landmarks (eye corners,
eyelids, brows, nose, mouth, face oval) plus 52 expression scores. Guidance, blink, framing,
light and sharpness sampling and the identity comparison all read the same landmark set.

Identity is a shape print, not a biometric embedding: 39 landmarks on a fixed stride, reduced
to position-, scale-, roll- and pixel-format-invariant coordinates, stored as five unit-length
78-value samples under template model ID mesh-print-v1. MediaPipe publishes no face recognition
or embedding task in tasks-vision 1.1.1, so no enrolled-biometric accuracy claim applies to it
and none is made here. Close relatives are expected to measure nearer than strangers.

Presentation defence is the blink: the eyes must close and reopen with natural timing while the
face stays framed, which a printed photograph cannot do. A trained video replay can, and the
separate MiniFASNet anti-spoof model previously used here has been removed.

Camera frames and landmark lists exist only in memory during the check. The landmarks are not
stored, sent to the server or written to disk; only the 78-value print is encrypted on the phone
and enrolled. MediaPipe's own privacy notice applies: https://goo.gle/mediapipe-privacy.

Removed on this rewrite, with their licences and checksums: YuNet (yunet.onnx), SFace
(sface.onnx) and MiniFASNetV2 (minifasnet.onnx) from OpenCV Zoo and minivision-ai, together
with the OpenCV 4.12 Android dependency. Any registration made with them is unreadable to this
build and needs supervised re-enrollment.

These models are not Aadhaar FaceRD, certified liveness detectors, or evidence of measured
deployment accuracy. Physical-phone and population/attack testing is required.
