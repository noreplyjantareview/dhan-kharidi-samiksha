Overview

How to read this

Present = checked in inside their kendra geofence during their own shift. Absent = staff with no check-in today. Presence pulses re-verify that staff stay on duty; the logs tab shows pulse counts and day-quality flags per person per day.

Staff vs nodal

Staff report to one kendra on a shift you set when you assign it — check-in opens for 30 minutes, arrivals over 15 minutes late are flagged. Nodal officers may visit several kendras with no fixed time: each face-checked arrival is stamped in the Nodal visits report and closed by the last pulse seen there.

A nodal visit opens when the officer marks On Duty with a face check inside the kendra geofence, and closes at the last presence pulse seen there. Time on site is arrival → last pulse.

Enroll staff

Employee code is required and must be unique. Shift timing is set later, per person, from Assign.

Add kendra

A kendra is only a place: location, geofence radius and how often presence is re-verified. Shift timing belongs to each staff member and is set from Staff → Assign.

Bulk enroll from Excel

Columns: Full name, Employee code, Mobile number, Role (staff/admin), Category (staff/nodal), Kendra name(s) (comma-separated for nodal), Check-in time and Day ends (optional, for staff). Kendras must already exist.
